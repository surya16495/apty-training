Shang-Chi and the Legend of the Ten Rings

"Shang-Chi and the Legend of the Ten Rings" introduces Shang-Chi, who left his father's powerful Ten Rings organization. Living quietly, his past catches up when assassins attack. Shang-Chi and his friend Katy find his estranged sister Xialing, only to be captured by their father, Xu Wenwu. Wenwu, wielding the Ten Rings, is deluded into believing his deceased wife, Ying Li, is trapped in the mystical village of Ta Lo, manipulated by the ancient Dweller-in-Darkness. After escaping to Ta Lo, they learn the truth. In a climactic battle, Shang-Chi confronts Wenwu, who breaches the Dark Gate, unleashing the Dweller-in-Darkness. Wenwu sacrifices himself, passing the Ten Rings to Shang-Chi, who then defeats the entity. Shang-Chi becomes a new MCU hero, as Wong, Bruce Banner, and Captain Marvel investigate the Ten Rings' mysterious signal. Xialing takes control of the organization, hinting at future developments. The film is a thrilling blend of martial arts, family drama, and mythology.
